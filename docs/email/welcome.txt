You're all set with Kilroy.

Kilroy tells you when the emails you send in Gmail get opened. Here's what to
know — the parts the extension can't fit next to a button.

USING IT
1. Compose a message in Gmail. A "Tracking" chip appears next to Send.
2. Send it. When it's opened, a double-check and a count appear on your sent
   message, right in the thread.
3. Open the dashboard from the Kilroy toolbar icon for opens per day, opens by
   recipient, and every message you've tracked.

WHAT AN "OPEN" REALLY MEANS
An open is an image being fetched -- not proof a person read your email. Kilroy
sets aside the things that fetch images but aren't people: security scanners,
Apple Mail's privacy prefetch, and your own re-reads. It never invents a city or
a device. Treat the first-open time as the reliable number; counts are a floor,
not a total.

YOUR DATA IS YOURS
Your tracking is scoped to your account and isolated from every other user. Want
to hold it entirely yourself? One click in the options points Kilroy at your own
database, and then nobody, not even us, can read it.

GOOD TO KNOW
- Turn tracking off for any single message from the chip beside Send.
- Pin Kilroy: click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar, then the pin next
  to Kilroy, so the dashboard is one click away.
- Using your own database? Export your settings (Options > Preferences) so a
  reinstall can't lose them.

Open Gmail and try it: https://mail.google.com/
